## Design Context
### Users
Chinese-speaking players who want to open a browser and immediately play classic Plants vs. Zombies, using a mouse or touch.
### Brand Personality
Nostalgic, playful, botanical. Preserve the recognizable classic lawn and animated characters.
### Aesthetic Direction
Warm ivory background, forest green navigation and panels, lime highlights, cream seed packets, original daytime garden art. Chinese interface with small editorial English labels. The playable garden is the visual center.
### Design Principles
- Familiar gameplay and instant access.
- Readable seed prices, cooldowns, selection, and wave feedback.
- Locally bundled assets and no runtime network dependencies.
- Responsive pointer and touch interaction, visible focus, keyboard shortcuts.
